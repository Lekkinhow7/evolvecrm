Small pill for status and meta labels — status on rows, period/meta in card headers.

```jsx
<Badge tone="danger">Overdue</Badge>
<Badge tone="active">In progress</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">7 days</Badge>
```

- Status copy is lower-case-first sentence ("Due soon", "within limits"), never ALL CAPS.
- Meta pills (period, "live", counts) always tone="neutral" in the top-right of a Panel.
